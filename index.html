About JCTC

A coordinated response to cybercrime.

JCTC brings together relevant criminal justice and government stakeholders to strengthen collaboration in preventing, investigating and prosecuting cybercrime in Nigeria.

⚖️

Strengthen Justice

Support an effective criminal justice response to cybercrime and related digital offences.

🤝

Build Partnerships

Promote cooperation among government, law enforcement, justice-sector and societal stakeholders.

🛡️

Protect the Public

Raise awareness so people can identify risks, protect themselves and report cybercrime appropriately.
